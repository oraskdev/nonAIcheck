My balcony garden plan

I’m planning to grow herbs on my balcony this spring so I can have fresh ingredients available when I’m cooking. The balcony gets direct sunlight in the morning, but the neighboring building shades it in the afternoon. I still need to measure the exact amount of sunlight before choosing all the plants. Basil, mint, and parsley are my first choices, though that list may change once I learn more about what they need.

Space is limited, and I don’t want the pots to make it difficult to open the balcony door. I intend to start with 3 containers rather than fill the whole area immediately, which should allow me to learn how much maintenance is required before making a larger commitment. I also need to check that the containers have drainage holes and that any water underneath them won’t cause problems for the downstairs neighbor.

My budget for this first stage is $60, and that amount needs to cover the containers, potting mix, and the plants themselves. I already have a small watering can and don’t expect to purchase another. If the total comes out higher than I expect, I’ll buy fewer plants rather than increase the budget. I want this to be a manageable experiment, not an expensive project.

Watering is what I’m most unsure about. I sometimes travel for work, and I can’t count on a neighbor always being available to help. Before buying anything, I’ll think through what would happen if I were away for 4 days. A simple self-watering container might help, but I haven’t compared prices or checked whether one would suit the plants I want to grow.

I hope to begin on 18 April, as long as the weather is suitable and I’ve finished the necessary checks. After the first month, I’ll review which plants are doing well and whether I enjoy the routine. If the experiment works out, I may add another herb later. For now, I just want to learn and grow a small amount of food without taking on more than I can comfortably maintain.
