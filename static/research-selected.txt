My balcony garden plan

I’m planning to grow herbs on my balcony this spring so I have fresh ingredients available when I cook. It gets direct sunlight in the morning, but the neighboring building shades it in the afternoon. I haven’t measured exactly how much sunlight it gets yet, and I need to do that before choosing all the plants. I’m leaning toward basil, mint, and parsley, though I may change that list after learning more about what they need.

I plan to start with 3 containers instead of filling the balcony right away, so I can find out how much care they need before adding more. There isn’t much space, and I don’t want the pots to get in the way of opening the balcony door. I still need to check that the containers have drainage holes and that water underneath them won’t cause problems for the downstairs neighbor.

Watering is the part I’m least sure about. I sometimes travel for work, so I can’t assume a neighbor will always be available to help. Before I buy anything, I’ll think through what would happen if I were away for 4 days. A simple self-watering container might help, but I haven’t compared prices or checked whether it would suit the plants I want.

I have a budget of $60 for this first stage, including containers, potting mix, and plants. I already own a small watering can and don’t expect to buy another. If the total costs more than I expect, I’ll buy fewer plants rather than raise the budget. I want a manageable experiment, not an expensive project.

I hope to start on 18 April, if the weather is suitable and I’ve finished the checks I need to make. After the first month, I’ll look at which plants are doing well and whether I’m enjoying the routine. If it works out, I may add another herb later. For now, my goal is to learn and grow a small amount of food without taking on more than I can comfortably care for.
